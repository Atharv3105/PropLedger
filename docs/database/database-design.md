# PropLedger — Relational Database Architecture Baseline
## Schema Design, Entity Normalization, Constraints, and History Model

---

## 1. Relational Schema Architecture

The PropLedger database models residential and commercial real estate operations across 8 primary domain modules and 2 cross-cutting audit subsystems.

### Core Domain Clusters

1. **Authentication & Access Control**:
   - `users`: User identity, credential hash (bcrypt), active state, contact email.
   - `roles`: System roles (`ADMIN`, `PROPERTY_MANAGER`, `LEASING_STAFF`, `ACCOUNTANT`, `MAINTENANCE_STAFF`, `OWNER`, `TENANT`).
   - `permissions`: Fine-grained actions (`property:create`, `payment:record`, `report:financial:view`, etc.).
   - `user_roles`: Many-to-many junction linking users to active roles.
   - `role_permissions`: Many-to-many junction mapping roles to authorized permissions.

2. **Property & Asset Hierarchy**:
   - `owners`: Legal entity or individual property owner records.
   - `properties`: Real estate parcels (Residential, Commercial, Mixed), address, total area, ownership percentage.
   - `buildings`: Physical structures located within a property campus.
   - `units`: Individual rentable units (Studio, 1BHK, 2BHK, 3BHK, Commercial Office, Retail Suite), market rent, target rent.
   - `unit_statuses`: Lookup catalog (`AVAILABLE`, `OCCUPIED`, `RESERVED`, `MAINTENANCE`, `INACTIVE`).

3. **Tenant & Contact Management**:
   - `tenants`: Primary tenant profile, personal ID reference, tax number, credit rating.
   - `tenant_contacts`: Multi-record contact book (Phone, Email, Emergency Contact, Employer).

4. **Leasing Operations**:
   - `late_fee_policies`: Policy configuration (Grace period days, flat fee amount, percentage rate, max fee cap).
   - `leases`: Lease legal contracts, unit ID, start date, end date, rent due day, monthly rent, security deposit.
   - `lease_tenants`: Many-to-many junction associating primary tenants and co-signers with leases.
   - `lease_statuses`: Lookup catalog (`DRAFT`, `ACTIVE`, `EXPIRING`, `EXPIRED`, `TERMINATED`, `RENEWED`).

5. **Billing & Payment Engine**:
   - `rent_charges`: Periodic rent assessments generated by billing batch (`usp_GenerateMonthlyRent`).
   - `late_fees`: Assessed late fee penalties linked to overdue rent charges.
   - `payments`: Transactional payment records (Method, Reference Number, Timestamp, Total Amount).
   - `payment_allocations`: Line-item breakdown applying payment funds to specific rent charges or fees (FIFO).
   - `tenant_balances`: Running financial ledger snapshot per tenant/lease for fast O(1) balance retrieval.

6. **Collections & Delinquency**:
   - `collection_cases`: Formal delinquency tracking files for accounts with >30 days unpaid balance.
   - `collection_activities`: Audit log of collection efforts (Phone calls, demand letters, legal notices).

7. **Maintenance & Vendor Operations**:
   - `vendors`: External contractor profiles, service trade (Plumbing, Electrical, HVAC, Roofing), contact info.
   - `maintenance_requests`: Tenant- or manager-reported issues, priority (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `EMERGENCY`).
   - `work_orders`: Actionable dispatches assigned to technicians or vendors, materials cost, labor cost.

8. **Accounting & Financial Reporting**:
   - `invoices`: Accounts Payable vendor invoices for goods and services.
   - `invoice_items`: Line-item expense details.
   - `expenses`: General property operating expenses (Utilities, Taxes, Insurance, Landscaping).
   - `security_deposits`: Escrow ledger tracking tenant deposit deposits, deductions, and refunds.

9. **Auditing & Historical State Preservation (Rule L & Part L)**:
   - `audit_logs`: General system mutation log (Table, Record ID, Action, Old Values, New Values, Changed By, Timestamp).
   - `lease_history`: Historical snapshot of lease modifications, rent amendments, and renewals.
   - `payment_audit`: Immutable audit trail of every recorded payment, partial allocation, and adjustment.
   - `status_history`: Historical log of all entity status transitions (Units, Leases, Maintenance Requests).

## 2. Normalization & Integrity Strategy

### Normalization Level: Third Normal Form (3NF)
- **1NF**: All columns contain atomic values. Multi-tenant relationships and multiple contacts are separated into junction tables (`lease_tenants`, `tenant_contacts`).
- **2NF**: Every non-key column is fully functionally dependent on the entire primary key. No partial key dependencies.
- **3NF**: Non-key columns depend only on primary keys, not on other non-key attributes (Transitive dependencies removed).
- **Controlled Denormalization**: `tenant_balances` maintains a running total balance maintained by transactional procedures and triggers, eliminating expensive continuous full-table historical scans for balance checks.

### Integrity Constraints
1. **Primary Keys**: Surrogate integer IDs (`BIGSERIAL`) or UUIDs for all primary entities.
2. **Foreign Keys**: Enforced referential integrity across all relationships with `ON DELETE RESTRICT` on financial transactions to prevent accidental cascade deletions.
3. **Check Constraints**:
   - `leases_date_check`: `CHECK (start_date <= end_date)`
   - `payments_amount_check`: `CHECK (amount > 0)`
   - `rent_charges_amount_check`: `CHECK (amount >= 0)`
   - `units_market_rent_check`: `CHECK (market_rent >= 0)`
   - `late_fees_amount_check`: `CHECK (amount >= 0)`
4. **Uniqueness Constraints**:
   - `users_email_unique`: `UNIQUE (email)`
   - `units_property_code_unique`: `UNIQUE (building_id, unit_number)`
   - `properties_code_unique`: `UNIQUE (property_code)`

## 3. Standard Audit Columns Policy

Every mutable domain table incorporates standard audit columns:

```sql
created_by   BIGINT       NOT NULL REFERENCES users(user_id),
created_at   TIMESTAMPTZ  NOT NULL DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
modified_by  BIGINT       REFERENCES users(user_id),
modified_at  TIMESTAMPTZ  DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
```

Automated `BEFORE UPDATE` triggers maintain `modified_at = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP` and verify `modified_by`.
